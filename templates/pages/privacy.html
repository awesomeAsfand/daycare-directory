Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains what information DaycaresPK ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

You can use DaycaresPK without creating an account, and the site has no forms that ask for your personal details. Like most websites, our servers automatically record basic technical information for each request: your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit, searches you make on the site and the time of your visit. We use these logs to keep the site running and secure, and to understand which pages are useful. If you email us, we use your email only to reply to you.

Cookies and advertising

DaycaresPK is funded by advertising served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site works without them.

Embedded maps

Listing pages include an embedded Google Map. When it loads, Google may collect information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Information about listed centres

Listings contain business information about childcare centres that is publicly available, such as their Google Maps listings. Reviews shown on listings are public Google reviews and appear with the reviewer's public display name. Centre owners and reviewers can ask us to correct or remove information through our contact page.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the services that run the site (our hosting provider, and Google for advertising and maps as described above), or where the law requires it.

Children

DaycaresPK is meant for parents and carers. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
