About DaycaresPK

DaycaresPK is a free directory that helps parents find and compare daycares, preschools and Montessori centres in Islamabad. Listings are organised by sector and area, so you can see what is close to home or work, compare ratings and opening hours, and contact centres directly.

What we list

We list daycare centres and standalone preschools and Montessori centres for young children. We don't list schools, including schools that run their own Montessori or pre-primary section, tuition centres or other businesses. We currently cover Islamabad; other cities may follow.

Where the information comes from

Listings are compiled from publicly available business information, mainly each centre's public Google Maps listing: name, address, phone number, website, opening hours, photos, rating and public reviews. We check each place's category and location and leave out places that aren't childcare centres or are outside Islamabad.

Details can change. Always confirm fees, hours, availability and anything else that matters to you with the centre before visiting or enrolling.

Independence

DaycaresPK is independent and is not affiliated with any of the centres listed, or with Google. A listing is not an endorsement. Listings marked Featured are paid placements; listings marked Verified are centres that have confirmed their details with us. The site is funded by advertising.

For centre owners

If you run a centre and want to correct your details, add photos, claim your listing or have it removed, please contact us.
