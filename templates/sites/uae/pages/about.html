About {{ site.name }}

{{ site.name }} is a free directory that helps parents find and compare nurseries and early learning centres in the UAE. Listings are organised by emirate and community, so you can see what is close to home or work, compare ratings and opening hours, and contact nurseries directly.

What we list

We list nurseries and early learning centres for babies and young children. We don't list schools, including schools that run their own FS1, FS2 or KG classes, or tuition centres and other businesses. We are starting with Dubai; the other emirates will follow.

Where the information comes from

Listings are compiled from publicly available business information, mainly each nursery's public Google Maps listing: name, address, phone number, website, opening hours, photos, rating and public reviews. We check each place's category and location and leave out places that aren't nurseries or early learning centres.

Details can change. Always confirm fees, hours, availability, licensing and anything else that matters to you with the nursery before visiting or enrolling.

Independence

{{ site.name }} is independent and is not affiliated with any of the nurseries listed, or with Google. A listing is not an endorsement. Listings marked Featured are paid placements; listings marked Verified are nurseries that have confirmed their details with us. The site is funded by advertising.

For nursery owners

If you run a nursery and want to correct your details, add photos, claim your listing or have it removed, please contact us.
